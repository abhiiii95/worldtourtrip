# Product: World Tour Trip

A travel and tourism website for Indian travelers. It surfaces curated travel packages, destinations, and blog content to help users discover and plan trips across India and abroad.

## Core Sections
- **Home** — banner, featured packages, destinations, blog highlights, newsletter signup
- **Destinations** — browsable destination listings with detail pages
- **Packages** — tour packages with booking cards and photo galleries
- **Blog** — travel articles with detail pages and recent post sidebars
- **About / Contact / FAQ** — informational and lead-capture pages
- **Privacy / Terms** — legal pages

## Domain
Production site: `https://www.worldtourtrip.com`

## Target Audience
Indian travelers looking for weekend getaways, honeymoon packages, and domestic/international tour packages.
